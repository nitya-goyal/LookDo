Why Gemma 4?

Gemma receives image + language together and resolves which visible UI element best matches the user's intent. Its structured coordinates drive the highlight.

Voice is input, not the reasoning layer. Browser SpeechRecognition fills the intent field; the user reviews it and clicks Locate Element or Guide Me.
